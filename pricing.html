FAQs

Pricing questions

Something we haven't covered?No sales pitch. Just a live walkthrough tailored to accounting firms.
What counts as a transaction?

A transaction is a single line Ledger AI extracts and categorises from a client document, such as one entry on a bank statement.

What happens if I go over my allowance?

On Lite and Standard, additional transactions are billed at 2.5p each and additional clients at £2 per client per month. On Premium, connect with us and we will tailor the allowance to your firm.

Is the email agent included on every plan?

Yes. The email agent, data extraction, organisation and categorisation are included on Lite, Standard and Premium.

How much do I save by paying yearly?

Yearly billing costs roughly ten months of the monthly price: £99 instead of £119.88 on Lite, £999 instead of £1,188 on Standard and £1,999 instead of £2,388 on Premium.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Lite and Standard keep your data for one month after cancellation so you can export it. Premium offers custom and extended retention options.

Can I get custom integrations?

Custom integrations are available on Premium. Connect with us and we will scope what your practice needs.
