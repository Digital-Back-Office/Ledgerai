Do you need an accountant for a startup?

There's no legal obligation to have an accountant if you're a new business, nor do you necessarily need one at first. You may be fine simply using accounting software. But bear in mind that you may need an accountant to help you submit annual returns for the company, manage payroll (if you have employees) and make sure you're compliant with tax and other rules.

What is the best accounting method for startups?

Each startup is different, but most find that at least some form of accounting software or the services of a professional accountant can be very useful. Spreadsheets can be complicated, time-consuming and at risk from human error, as well as not necessarily being Making Tax Digital (MTD) compliant.

Is Ledger AI compatible with Making Tax Digital?

Yes. Ledger AI produces MTD-ready digital records and posts directly into MTD-compatible software such as Xero, QuickBooks and Sage, keeping the required digital link between source documents and your VAT return.

Does Ledger AI replace my bookkeeper or accountant?

No. It removes the manual data entry and first-pass categorisation, but a person still reviews exceptions, signs off the books and handles client judgement calls. Most firms redeploy the time saved into advisory work rather than reducing headcount.

What is AI bookkeeping software?

AI bookkeeping software uses artificial intelligence and automation to assist with repetitive bookkeeping tasks such as transaction categorisation, reconciliation and document processing.

Is Ledger AI suitable for small accounting firms?

Yes. Automation can be particularly useful for smaller practices that want to increase capacity without adding unnecessary administrative workload.

How does Ledger AI help maintain accuracy?

Extraction and categorisation are checked before anything is posted: figures are validated against the source document, low-confidence or unclear items are flagged rather than posted silently, and every batch is reviewed by your team before it is exported to Xero, QuickBooks or Sage. Corrections your team makes feed back into the categorisation rules, so accuracy improves the more you use it.

What are the ideal use cases for Ledger AI?

Repetitive bookkeeping workflows: bank statement processing, invoice processing, transaction categorisation, and trial balance preparation for accounting firms and finance teams.
