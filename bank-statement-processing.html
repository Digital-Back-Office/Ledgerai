Bank Statement Processing

Automated Bank Statement Processing for UK Accounting Firms

Stop re-keying bank statements line by line. Ledger AI reads PDF, CSV and even scanned bank statements from any UK bank, extracts every transaction, categorises it against the client's chart of accounts, and reconciles it against the ledger — automatically.

What it does

Every transaction line, extracted and reconciled

Ledger AI ingests bank statements in whatever format they arrive — PDF exports, CSV downloads, scanned or photographed paper statements — and extracts the date, description, and amount of every transaction line.

It applies learned categorisation rules per client, matches transactions against the existing ledger, and reconciles the account, leaving only genuine exceptions for a bookkeeper to check.

Any statement format

PDF exports, CSV downloads, scans and photos of paper statements.

Per-client categorisation

Rules learned from each client's history and your team's corrections.

Exceptions only

Matched lines reconcile themselves; only genuine exceptions reach a human.

FAQs

Questions about Bank Statement Processing

Something we haven't covered?No sales pitch. Just a live walkthrough tailored to accounting firms.
Which banks and file formats are supported?

Ledger AI accepts CSV and PDF statement exports from all major UK banks, as well as open banking feeds where available, and can process scanned or photographed paper statements.

Can it read scanned or photographed statements?

Yes. The AI/OCR engine is built to handle low-quality scans and photos, not just clean digital exports.

How does the system learn categorisation rules?

It learns from each client's transaction history and any corrections your team makes, so accuracy improves the more you use it.

What happens if a transaction is unclear?

Unclear or low-confidence transactions are flagged and held for manual review rather than posted automatically, so nothing gets miscategorised silently.

Does it support multi-currency accounts?

Yes, multi-currency bank accounts and transactions are supported and reconciled in the client's base currency.
