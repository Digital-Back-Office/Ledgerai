How it works

From statement to report in minutes

  1. 01

    Receive client statements

    Connect your firm's inbox. Clients email their statements to you as usual — PDFs, Excel files or photos.

  2. 02

    Statements are detected

    Ledger AI spots incoming statements from HSBC, Barclays, NatWest, Lloyds and all major UK banks, and maps each one to the correct client ledger.

  3. 03

    Transactions are extracted

    Every transaction is read from the document, parsed and de-duplicated. No manual data entry.

  4. 04

    Spending is categorised

    Each line is categorised — Revenue, HMRC VAT, Dividends, Travel, Payroll — by AI trained on UK accounting standards. Override any of them with one click.

  5. 05

    Trial balance is built

    A fully reconciled trial balance is generated, ready to drill into by category or by client.

  6. 06

    Review and export

    Your team is alerted the moment processing is complete. Check the figures, then export to Excel or PDF, or straight into Xero, QuickBooks or Sage.

What is Ledger AI?

Ledger AI is an AI-powered bookkeeping assistant that works in the background to capture incoming information, process bookkeeping tasks, and keep your workflow moving.

How is Ledger AI different from Xero, QuickBooks, and Sage?

Xero, QuickBooks, and Sage are accounting platforms. Ledger AI focuses on automating the workflow around bookkeeping — from inbox to books — while learning from historical data and patterns.

Can Ledger AI work with Xero, QuickBooks, and Sage?

Yes. Ledger AI is designed to work alongside your existing accounting software, helping automate bookkeeping without replacing the tools you already use.

Can Ledger AI integrate with other systems?

Yes. Ledger AI can integrate with other finance and business tools through supported integrations and APIs.

Can Ledger AI automatically ingest emails and documents?

Yes. The Ledger AI Email Agent can capture relevant emails, invoices, receipts, and financial documents and bring them into the bookkeeping workflow automatically.

Does Ledger AI learn from historical data?

Yes. Ledger AI can learn from available historical data and patterns to help inform future bookkeeping workflows.

Is my data secure?

Ledger AI is designed with security and data protection in mind, with safeguards in place to protect your financial information.

Does Ledger AI replace accountants?

No. Ledger AI handles repetitive workflow activity in the background, while accountants provide the review, context, judgement, and expertise.
