Ledger AI reads every invoice your clients send — PDF, scanned, or forwarded by email — and extracts the supplier, date, line items, VAT and totals automatically, posting them straight into the ledger with the correct nominal code and VAT treatment.

## Automated Invoice Processing Built for Accounting Practices

Most automated invoice processing software on the market is built for large enterprises with dedicated finance IT teams and long implementation projects. Ledger AI is built the other way round — for UK accounting practices and small businesses that need accurate invoice data extraction and VAT handling from day one, with no procurement process required.

## What It Does

Ledger AI extracts structured data from purchase and sales invoices regardless of format or supplier layout. It captures supplier details, invoice and due dates, line-item descriptions, net amounts, VAT rates and totals, matches invoices to existing suppliers or purchase orders, flags likely duplicates, and posts approved invoices directly to the ledger.

## FAQs

### Can it process handwritten or scanned invoices?

Yes. The AI/OCR engine is trained on scanned, photographed and handwritten invoices, in addition to native digital PDFs.

### Does it handle multi-currency or overseas invoices?

Yes, foreign currency invoices are extracted and converted to the client's base currency for posting.

### How does it handle different VAT schemes?

Ledger AI recognises standard, flat rate, and reverse charge VAT treatment and applies the correct code based on the client's registered scheme.

### How accurate is duplicate invoice detection?

The system compares supplier, invoice number, date and amount across historical records to flag likely duplicates before they're posted.

### Can the approval workflow be customised?

Yes, approval routing can be configured per client — for example, requiring sign-off above a certain value or for new suppliers.
