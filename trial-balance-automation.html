What it does

From categorised transactions to a review-ready trial balance

Once transactions are categorised — through bookkeeping automation or manually — Ledger AI pulls everything together into a formatted trial balance per client. It applies standard adjusting entries such as accruals, prepayments and depreciation templates, checks that the TB balances, flags unusual variances or missing entries against prior periods, and exports a review-ready file to your accounts production software.

Does this remove the need for manual trial balance review?

No — it removes the repetitive checking work, so reviewers can focus their time on genuine judgement calls and client-specific issues rather than chasing basic errors.

How are adjusting entries like accruals and depreciation handled?

You set up templates per client (or use standard defaults) and Ledger AI applies them consistently each period, with a log of every adjustment made.

Can I customise the chart of accounts mapping?

Yes, chart of accounts mapping is fully configurable per client and can follow your practice's standard nominal structure.
